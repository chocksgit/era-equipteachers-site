Frequently Asked Questions

Is ERA an official CLEP® exam?

No. ERA is an independent readiness assessment and is not an official exam.

Will this guarantee a passing score?

No. ERA helps indicate readiness and identify areas for improvement. It does not guarantee official exam performance.

How long will the assessment take?

Most readiness assessments are expected to take about 20–40 minutes depending on the subject.
