Frequently Asked Questions

Is ERA an official exam?

No. ERA is an independent readiness assessment designed to help students prepare more effectively.

Does ERA guarantee a score?

No assessment can guarantee an official exam score. ERA helps indicate readiness and identify areas for improvement.

How long does an assessment take?

Most readiness assessments are designed to be completed in about 20–45 minutes, depending on the exam.
