Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official CLEP® exam?

No. ERA is an independent readiness assessment and is not an official CLEP exam.

Will this guarantee a passing score?

No. It indicates readiness and identifies focus areas. It does not guarantee official exam performance.

How long does it take?

Most readiness assessments are designed to be completed in one focused sitting.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our team will follow up with access details and next steps.
